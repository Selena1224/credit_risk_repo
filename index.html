SOURCE: four user-supplied CSV datasets. The historical dataset includes 425 borrowers, the held-out evaluation includes 85 cases, and prospective scoring includes 10 borrowers. Charts update locally in your browser. Plotly is loaded from a CDN, so an internet connection is required for interactive charts. Missingness, class balance, subgroup sample sizes, calibration, and potential fairness concerns should be reviewed before any operational use. No model is trained or refit in this dashboard.
